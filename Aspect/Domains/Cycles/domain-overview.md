# Cycles (dom.cycles)

## Summary
Supports team iteration management with timeboxed cycles to track progress on issues and goals.
Helps maintain team momentum and visualize project timelines.

## Users
- Scrum masters and team leads: create and manage cycles.
- Team members: track progress during cycles.

## Core Capabilities
- Creating, starting, and closing cycles.
- Visual progress tracking (e.g., burn-down charts).
- Associating issues and goals to cycles.
- Reporting on cycle metrics.

## Key Pages & Entry Points
- `/cycles`, `/cycle/[id]`
- API routes `/api/cycles/*`

## Representative Code
- Cycle components in frontend and backend APIs.

## Related Entities
`Cycle`, `Issue`, `Goal`

## Dependencies
- Depends on Issues and Goals domains.

## Product & Business Value
Improves team productivity and sprint management.
